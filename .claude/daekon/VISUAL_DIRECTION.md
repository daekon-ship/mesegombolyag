# Mesegombolyag — Visual Direction

Status: **draft concept, not yet approved by the client.**

## Grounding

Before designing, Johanna's own saved Instagram export (`(1) Instagram_files/`) was inspected. Her existing graphic posts already use a warm cream-paper background, a deep forest-green handwritten/script headline treatment, terracotta-brown serif body copy, and delicate sage botanical line-art accents. The palette and motif choices below extend that existing brand voice rather than inventing a new one — this is a deliberate choice so the concept reads as "hers," not as a generic new identity imposed on her.

## Typography

- Display/headings: **Fraunces** (editorial serif, matches the brief's request and echoes her own serif body copy).
- Script accent (sparingly — subheads, one pull-quote): **Caveat**, standing in for her own handwritten-script Instagram headlines without copying a specific font.
- Body/UI: **Manrope**.

## Motifs (2–3 as instructed, not all at once)

1. **Fonal / gombolyag (thread)** — `ThreadDivider`, `HeroThread`: organic hand-drawn curve, draws on scroll.
2. **Gyökér és ág (root & branch)** — `BranchMotif`: botanical line art, directly echoing the branch illustrations already in Johanna's own Instagram graphics.
3. **Kör (circle)** — organic asymmetric blob-masked portrait crops (hero, about), plus the small dot that terminates each thread.
